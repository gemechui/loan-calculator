Repayment Capacity

AB Bank staff: basic basis = ½ of basic salary minus fringe tax (10%); net basis = net salary after income tax, pension and fringe tax. NGO staff: no fringe tax.

Spouse Income (Joint Assessment)

Spouse salary is always taken as his/her basic salary. Spouse contribution = ½ of spouse basic salary × share considered, added to the applicant's repayment capacity.
Eligibility: spouse basic salary must be greater than zero. Share can be 0–100% (default 100%).

Loan Terms

Interest is calculated on a reducing-balance (annuity) basis: rate per period = annual rate × (months per period ÷ 12). Maximum tenure: HL 25 years, PL 5 years, CL 15 years, and never beyond retirement at age 60. Salary basis: repayment capacity = ½ of monthly basic salary (less fringe tax for AB Bank staff), or the full net salary; net salary = basic − income tax (35% − 2,050) − pension (7%) − fringe tax (10%, AB Bank staff only). Spouse income is added as ½ of spouse basic salary × share considered. Figures are for appraisal guidance; confirm against current bank policy.
